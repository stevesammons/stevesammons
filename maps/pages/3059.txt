# Hebron and Jerusalem in Absalom’s Story
https://stevesammons.com/hebron-and-jerusalem-in-absaloms-story/

Absalom was born in Hebron, lived in Jerusalem, and launched his claim to kingship from Hebron. The map marks those secure centers rather than an uncertain battle route.
English reference map using public-domain Natural Earth data. Modern boundaries are orientation aids, not ancient borders; approximate markers preserve the limits of the evidence.
## How to read this map
Scripture gives no birth or death year. The duration in 2 Samuel 15:7 has a textual variant, and the exact location of the forest of Ephraim is uncertain.
