# Jerusalem in Nicodemus’s Story
https://stevesammons.com/jerusalem-in-nicodemuss-story/

All three appearances of Nicodemus in John are connected with Jesus’s ministry and death in Jerusalem.
English reference map using public-domain Natural Earth data. Modern boundaries are orientation aids, not ancient borders; approximate markers preserve the limits of the evidence.
## How to read this map
John does not give Nicodemus’s age, birth, death, or a complete account of his later life. Later church traditions are not used here to fill those gaps.
