# Galilee and Jerusalem in James’s Story
https://stevesammons.com/galilee-and-jerusalem-in-jamess-story/

James was called near the Sea of Galilee and was later killed during Herod Agrippa I’s rule in Jerusalem. The map gives regional orientation, not a reconstructed travel route.
English reference map using public-domain Natural Earth data. Modern boundaries are orientation aids, not ancient borders; approximate markers preserve the limits of the evidence.
## How to read this map
Scripture does not give James’s age or a detailed account of his work after Jesus’s resurrection. Later traditions about travel to Spain are not established by the New Testament.
