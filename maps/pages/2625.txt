## Place the story
Jerusalem sits in the hills roughly 32 miles east of the Mediterranean coast. This is geographic orientation, not a driving distance. The marker identifies the city, not a known council room or an exact escape route.
Ahithophel joined Absalom’s rebellion and advised him in Jerusalem. David had left the city. The debate over pursuing him appears in 2 Samuel 17:1–14.
Ahithophel’s hometown was elsewhere in Judah. Its exact modern identification is uncertain, so it is not plotted here.
