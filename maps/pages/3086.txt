# Harran and Bethlehem in Rachel’s Story
https://stevesammons.com/harran-and-bethlehem-in-rachels-story/

Rachel’s story begins near Harran and ends on the road near Ephrath, associated in Genesis with Bethlehem. The text does not preserve the family’s exact route.
English reference map using public-domain Natural Earth data. Modern boundaries are orientation aids, not ancient borders; approximate markers preserve the limits of the evidence.
## How to read this map
Genesis supplies no birth year, age, or secure calendar date for Rachel. The patriarchal chronology cannot responsibly be converted into an exact lifespan, and the location and later identification of Rachel’s tomb involve historical and interpretive questions.
