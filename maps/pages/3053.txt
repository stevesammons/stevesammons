# Babylon and Jerusalem in Ezra’s Story
https://stevesammons.com/babylon-and-jerusalem-in-ezras-story/

Ezra traveled from Babylon to Jerusalem. The book gives a four-month time span but no precise route; the map therefore marks endpoints, not an itinerary.
English reference map using public-domain Natural Earth data. Modern boundaries are orientation aids, not ancient borders; approximate markers preserve the limits of the evidence.
## How to read this map
Ezra’s birth and death years are unknown. The identity of the Artaxerxes in Ezra 7 and the ordering of Ezra and Nehemiah have been debated, so the calendar date is presented as the traditional reconstruction rather than certainty.
