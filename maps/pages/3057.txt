# Moab and Bethlehem in Naomi’s Story

Naomi traveled between Bethlehem and the country of Moab. Because Ruth does not name the Moabite settlement or route, the eastern marker is deliberately regional.
English reference map using public-domain Natural Earth data. Modern boundaries are orientation aids, not ancient borders; approximate markers preserve the limits of the evidence.
## How to read this map
Ruth does not provide Naomi’s birth year, death year, age, or the precise place in Moab where the family lived. The judges period spans competing chronologies, so no invented lifespan is supplied.
