## The rebuilding Zechariah knew
His dated messages begin around 520 BC, during Persian rule. The community was rebuilding the temple destroyed by Babylon. Ezra 6 describes its completion. This rebuilding began the temple’s next chapter, centuries before King Herod enlarged the complex familiar from the Gospel accounts.
## Reading today’s city
The Old City’s eastern edge and the valley beyond help locate the site. Modern walls, gates, streets, and religious buildings belong to many later periods. The Dome of the Rock was not part of Zechariah’s surroundings. The map provides geographic orientation; it does not reconstruct Jerusalem in his lifetime.
