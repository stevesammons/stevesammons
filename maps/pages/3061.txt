# Jerusalem in Bathsheba’s Story

Bathsheba’s recorded story is centered in Jerusalem. The Ammonite capital Rabbah, where Uriah died, lay east of the Jordan near modern Amman.
English reference map using public-domain Natural Earth data. Modern boundaries are orientation aids, not ancient borders; approximate markers preserve the limits of the evidence.
## How to read this map
Scripture does not give Bathsheba’s birth year, death year, private thoughts, or ability to refuse David’s summons. Her exact ancestry and later years remain uncertain.
