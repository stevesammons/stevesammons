# Shechem in Abimelech’s Story
https://stevesammons.com/shechem-in-abimelechs-story/

Shechem was the city whose people supported Abimelech’s rise. Its archaeological site, Tell Balata, lies about two kilometres—roughly a mile—east-southeast of the center of Nablus in today’s West Bank. The marker identifies the ancient city site, not the exact place of his crowning or a particular battle.
English reference map using public-domain Natural Earth boundary and coastline data. The marker identifies Tell Balata as the usual archaeological orientation point, not a reconstructed scene.
## A city in the hills
The ancient city occupied a valley between two mountains. Its position connected travel routes and farmland. Archaeologists have found walls, gates, and religious buildings from several periods. Those remains establish a long history of settlement; they do not identify every event in Judges 9.
## Reading the story and the map
Judges follows Abimelech beyond Shechem as well as within it. This map focuses on the city that supported him and that he later destroyed. It is not a route map of his campaigns.
Modern roads and neighborhoods help readers find the location. They should not be pictured as part of the ancient scene. The portrait’s gate recess is plausible context, not a photograph of a verified surviving gate from his reign.
