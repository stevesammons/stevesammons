# The Sea of Galilee in Andrew’s Story
https://stevesammons.com/the-sea-of-galilee-in-andrews-story/

Andrew came from Bethsaida and worked in the Sea of Galilee region. Exact shoreline locations and the identification of ancient Bethsaida remain debated.
English reference map using public-domain Natural Earth data. Modern boundaries are orientation aids, not ancient borders; approximate markers preserve the limits of the evidence.
## How to read this map
The New Testament does not record Andrew’s birth, death, or later ministry after Acts 1. Traditions about Scythia, Greece, and an X-shaped cross are later and are not treated here as biblical fact.
